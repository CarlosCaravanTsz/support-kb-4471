How each report is placed

The cause comes from the tenant, not from intake’s tag. Intake tags from the reporter’s browser; the cause depends on where the tenant is and which booking form it runs (causes.ts, locales.ts).

  1. hint if it came through the form of a day-first tenant on booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type what it says, and the form reads the date day first.
  2. parser for every other report from a day-first tenant: the shared parser reads the date month first.
  3. shift, as well, if the desk read it as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). The date moves by one day, it does not flip, so the parser fix does not touch it.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can be several bugs that look alike. Merging on subject (decision 0001) puts them in one place; split them by cause before you mark the ticket known.
  • Count by report id, not by export row.
  • Place a report by its tenant, not by intake’s tag.
